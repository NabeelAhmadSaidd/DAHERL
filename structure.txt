# 📁 Project Structure & Module Overview

---

## 🔍 Detailed Module Breakdown

### 1. `ablations/` – Allocation Strategy Comparisons
Contains different allocation algorithms used as baselines or controlled experiments.
- `base_ablation.py` – Base class for all ablation strategies.
- `best_expert.py` – Always selects the historically best expert.
- `full_daherl.py` – Full DAHERL allocator (the main proposal).
- `no_diversity.py` – Allocator without diversity penalty.
- `no_regime.py` – Allocator without regime information.
- `random_allocator.py` – Random expert selection (control).

### 2. `allocator/` – The Meta‑Agent
The RL‑based allocator that dynamically selects which expert to follow.
- `allocator_agent.py` – The RL agent (policy network, action selection).
- `allocator_env.py` – Custom Gym‑like environment wrapping the experts.
- `train_allocator.py` – Training script for the allocator.
- `allocator_evaluation_notebook.ipynb` – Notebook for analysing allocator performance.

### 3. `checkpoints/` – Pre‑Trained Model Weights
- `EURUSD/` – Expert weights for the EURUSD asset:
  `ppg_expert.pt`, `ddqn_expert.pt`, `ppo_expert.pt`, `sac_expert.pt`, `td3_expert.pt`, `tqc_expert.pt`
- `regime/` – Regime detection model:
  `EURUSD_tcn_multi.pth` (TCN trained on EURUSD)

### 4. `configs/` – Central Configuration
- `ablation.yaml` – Parameters for ablation studies.
- `allocator.yaml` – Allocator training hyperparameters.
- `data.yaml` – Data sources, symbols, timeframes.
- `experts.yaml` – Expert training settings.

### 5. `data/` – Financial Time Series
- `raw/` – Original hourly OHLCV data:
  `BTCUSDT_H1.csv`, `ETHUSDT_H1.csv`, `EURUSD_H1.csv`, `USDJPY_H1.csv`
- `processed/` – Feature‑engineered splits (`train.csv`, `test.csv`) for each symbol.

### 6. `diversity/` – Ensuring Expert Uniqueness
- `diversity_manager.py` – Orchestrates diversity calculations and penalties.
- `diversity_wrapper.py` – Wraps the environment to inject diversity reward/penalty.
- `metrics.py` – Statistical metrics (e.g., correlation, action disagreement).
- `penalty.py` – Computes penalty based on metric values.
- `reward_wrapper.py` – Modifies reward with diversity term.
- `storage.py` – Serialisation/deserialisation of diversity results.
- `measure_diversity.ipynb` – Interactive notebook to explore diversity.
- `tests/test_diversity.py` – Unit tests for the diversity module.

### 7. `environment/` – Trading Simulation
- `trading_env.py` – The core trading environment (step, reset, observation space).
- `reward_functions.py` – Different reward formulations (e.g., Sharpe, returns, differential Sharpe).

### 8. `eval_notebooks/` – Expert Evaluation
Individual Jupyter notebooks that load a trained expert and visualise its performance:
`ppg.ipynb`, `ddqn.ipynb`, `ppo.ipynb`, `sac.ipynb`, `td3.ipynb`, `tqc.ipynb`

### 9. `experiments/` – Running Ablation Studies
- `run_ablation.py` – Executes a single ablation configuration.
- `compare_ablations.py` – Aggregates results from all ablations and produces comparisons.

### 10. `experts/` – Individual Trading Agents
Implementations of six reinforcement learning algorithms, all inheriting from `base_agent.py`.
- `ppg_expert.py`, `ddqn_expert.py`, `ppo_expert.py`, `sac_expert.py`, `td3_expert.py`, `tqc_expert.py`
- `networks.py` – Neural network architectures (actors, critics).
- `train_experts.py` – Script to train all experts.
- `eval_agents.ipynb` – Notebook comparing multiple experts side‑by‑side.

### 11. `logs/` – Experiment Tracking
- `tensorboard/` – TensorBoard event files for each expert and allocator training run.
- `training/` – CSV logs of every episode (reward, portfolio value, etc.), separated by `experts` and `allocator`.

### 12. `preprocessing/` – Data Preparation
- `dataset_builder.py` – Creates train/test splits from raw data.
- `feature_engineering.py` – Computes technical indicators and normalises features.

### 13. `regime/` – Market Regime Detection
Uses a Temporal Convolutional Network (TCN) to classify market states (e.g., trending, ranging).
- `tcn_model.py` – TCN architecture.
- `train.py` – Training pipeline.
- `train_WV.py` – Alternative training (possibly with Word2Vec‑style embeddings).
- `inference.py` – Run regime prediction on new data.
- `dataset.py` – Custom dataset for regime labels.
- `config.py` – Hyperparameters for TCN training.
- `evaluate_regime.ipynb` – Evaluation and visualisation notebook.

### 14. `results/` – Trained Assets
- `models/regime/` – Label metadata (`.pkl`) for each symbol.
- `models/scalers/` – Saved scalers (`.pkl`) for each symbol.
- `regime/` – Summary CSV with regime statistics (e.g., `EURUSD_tcn_summary.csv`).

### 15. `scripts/` – Utilities
- `trainsingleasset.py` – Script to train an expert on a single asset (likely for quick tests).

### 16. `storage/` – Serialised Diversity Analysis
For EURUSD, stores pickled diversity results and summary CSVs:
- `PPG.pkl` … `TQC.pkl` – Per‑expert diversity data.
- `analysis/` – Aggregated diversity metrics (`diversity_summary.csv`, `diversity_performance.csv`, etc.).

### 17. `tests/` – Unit Tests
- `test_environment.py` – Validates the trading environment logic.
- (Inside `diversity/tests/`) `test_diversity.py` – Tests diversity modules.

---

## 🧩 Key Design Points
- **Modular:** Experts, allocator, regime detection, and diversity are all independent modules.
- **Multi‑Asset:** Supports EURUSD, BTCUSDT, ETHUSDT, USDJPY.
- **Ablation‑Ready:** Configs and dedicated scripts allow systematic comparison of design choices.
- **Extensive Logging:** TensorBoard and CSV logs for full reproducibility.
- **Pre‑Trained Assets:** Checkpoints provided for quick evaluation without retraining.

For more details, see `ablation_design.md`, `analysis.md`, and `status.md` in the root.
